Tailor vs manual resume tailoring

A neutral comparison to help you decide which workflow fits your job search.

Quick verdict

Manual tailoring offers full control but takes more time. Tailor speeds up the first draft while keeping you in control of edits and your AI usage.

Side-by-side comparison

Criteria Tailor Manual
Speed per application Seconds for first draft 20-60 minutes
Control over content High (edit before applying) Full
Workflow Built for LinkedIn Jobs Manual copy/edit
Cost model Free extension + your AI API usage Time cost only

When Tailor is the better fit

  • You apply to multiple roles weekly.
  • You want consistent, role-aligned drafts quickly.
  • You prefer a workflow inside LinkedIn Jobs.

When manual tailoring is best

  • You apply to a small number of high-stakes roles.
  • You want maximum customization on every line.
  • You prefer to avoid AI assistance entirely.
