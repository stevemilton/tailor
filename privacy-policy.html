Tailor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 February 2026

What Tailor does

Tailor is a Chrome extension that generates tailored CVs and cover letters for LinkedIn job listings using AI.

Data we collect

Tailor stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage:

  • Your LinkedIn profile URL
  • Your Claude API key
  • A text copy of your LinkedIn profile (used as your master CV)

This data never leaves your device except as described below.

Third-party services

When you click "Tailor My Application", the extension sends your profile text and the job description to the Anthropic API (api.anthropic.com) using your own API key to generate a tailored CV and cover letter. This is the only external service Tailor communicates with.

Anthropic's use of this data is governed by their privacy policy and API terms.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or tracking
  • No cookies
  • No data is sent to our servers (we don't have any)
  • No personal data is shared with anyone other than Anthropic when you generate an application

Data storage and deletion

All data is stored locally using chrome.storage.local. You can delete all stored data at any time by clicking the settings icon in the extension and resetting your setup, or by uninstalling the extension.

Permissions

  • activeTab / tabs – to read the current LinkedIn job listing
  • storage – to save your API key and profile locally
  • scripting – to read job details from the LinkedIn page
  • Host access to linkedin.com – to read job listings and your profile
  • Host access to api.anthropic.com – to generate tailored applications
