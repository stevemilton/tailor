FAQ

Clear answers for global job seekers.

Where is my data stored?

Your LinkedIn profile, AI API key, and master CV live in Chrome’s local storage on your device.

Do you provide the AI API key?

No. Tailor runs on your own AI API key, so you control usage, billing, and data.

What gets sent to the AI provider?

Only your profile text and the job description are sent to generate the tailored CV and cover letter.

Does Tailor work outside the U.S.?

Yes. Tailor works anywhere LinkedIn Jobs is available, with language shaped by the role text.

How much does it cost?

Tailor is free. You pay only your AI API usage, typically around £0.02–0.05 per application.
