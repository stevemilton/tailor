Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to help you decide if Tailor fits your job search.

Product and privacy

Do you provide the AI API key?

No. Tailor runs on your own AI API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, Cohere), so you control usage, billing, and data.

Where is my data stored?

Your LinkedIn profile, AI API key, and CV are stored locally in Chrome on your device. Supported providers: Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, Cohere.

What does Tailor send to the AI provider?

Only your profile text and the job description are sent to generate tailored documents.

Usage and compatibility

Does Tailor work outside the U.S.?

Yes. Tailor works wherever LinkedIn Jobs is available.

Do I need to be logged into LinkedIn?

Yes. Tailor reads your LinkedIn profile and job listings directly in your browser.

Can I edit before applying?

Yes. Tailor provides a draft that you can review and adjust before submitting.

Cost and control

Is Tailor free?

The extension is free; you pay only your AI API usage (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, Cohere).

How much does it cost per application?

Costs depend on your AI provider and prompt size. Tailor keeps usage under your control.

Can I remove all stored data?

Yes. Reset settings in the extension or uninstall it to clear local storage.
