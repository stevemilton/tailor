How to tailor your resume for LinkedIn jobs

A practical, repeatable workflow to improve alignment with each role without rewriting everything from scratch.

Step-by-step process

  1. Capture the requirements. Read the job post and write down the top 3-5 responsibilities and skills.
  2. Match your experience. Select relevant achievements that prove those skills.
  3. Rewrite the summary. Update your headline/summary to mirror the role’s language.
  4. Align skills. Reorder skills so the most relevant appear first.
  5. Review for clarity. Check accuracy, tone, and consistency before applying.

Common mistakes

  • Copying the job description verbatim without real evidence.
  • Inflating titles or responsibilities beyond reality.
  • Using a one-size-fits-all summary for every role.
