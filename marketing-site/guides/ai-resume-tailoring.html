What is AI resume tailoring?

AI resume tailoring is the process of adapting your resume and cover letter to a specific job description using AI assistance. It helps you align language, highlight the most relevant experience, and avoid generic applications.

How it works

  1. You provide a base resume (often from your LinkedIn profile).
  2. The AI reads the job description and identifies key requirements.
  3. It rewrites or reorders your resume content to match the role.
  4. You review and edit the output before applying.

What it’s good for

  • Speeding up high-volume job searches.
  • Ensuring the resume reflects the role’s language.
  • Reducing repetitive editing.

What to watch for

  • Accuracy — always verify dates, titles, and achievements.
  • Tone — keep your writing natural and honest.
  • Over-optimization — avoid stuffing keywords that don’t reflect your experience.

Tailor’s approach

Tailor is a browser extension that uses your own AI API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, Cohere) and your LinkedIn profile to generate role-specific CVs and cover letters. It is designed to keep data local and keep you in control of edits.
